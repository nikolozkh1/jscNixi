About JSC NIXI

Strategic thinking, applied with discipline.

Mission

To connect companies, investors, and institutions across borders with the strategy and relationships needed to succeed.

Vision

To be the trusted advisory bridge between Georgia, the EU, and the international markets that engage with them.

JSC NIXI Consultancy was founded to serve companies expanding across the Georgia–EU corridor and into wider international markets, at the intersection of strategy and execution.

Our team has built experience connecting companies with investors, aligning commercial partners across jurisdictions, and engaging the government and institutional stakeholders that international projects depend on — staying engaged from first conversation through to signed agreement.
